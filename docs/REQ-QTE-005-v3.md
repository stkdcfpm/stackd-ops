# REQ-QTE-005 — Customer-facing Quote document (and the internal costing sheet it must stop being confused with)

**Status:** v3 — resubmission after requirements-gate round 1 (FAIL: 3 blocking, 8 should-fix, 5 advisory; every finding mapped in §10). **One operator confirmation is still outstanding: the currency (§4 REQ-QTE5-005).**
**Type:** Feature + safety fix. **Phase 1 needs no schema change** (renders from existing quote fields). Phase 2 (stored issued copies) needs one additive Supabase column and is specified separately in §7 so Phase 1 can ship first.
**Operator decisions (2026-11-28):** per-line selling price; the three existing overhead lines (origin / destination / admin) are the separate charge lines — **enough as is**, free-form charge lines are not needed now; **USD** (the operator's answer to the currency question was cut off after "use" — read as USD; to be re-confirmed); a one-line **price basis** is printed; catalogue descriptions are clean of supplier references (the supplier-name safeguard in REQ-QTE5-006 stays as a backstop).
**Why now:** the operator needs to send a real container-load quote to a buyer and the app has no buyer-facing quote document.

---

## 1. The finding that triggered this

`prevQteDoc()` (`index.html:16155`, opened by the Quote modal's Preview button `3037`) is an **internal costing sheet**. Every line prints the **supplier name, cost, freight, insurance, duty and landed cost** (`index.html:16160-16170`, header row `16177`), the overhead charges are listed, and only the grand **Sell Price** is shown — in **both USD and GBP** (`index.html:16190-16191`) — followed by the quote **Notes** whenever present (`16193`). A buyer who receives it can compute FPM's margin (Sell − Landed − overhead) and can see exactly which factory to contact directly. It is not labelled internal. There is no per-line selling price, no payment terms, no bank details and no address header (its header prints `AS.name · AS.email` only, `index.html:16177`). Nothing is stored: the document is built in memory and opened in a new tab, so there is no record of what a buyer was actually sent (the invoice documents behave the same way, by design — `docs/user-guide.md` Invoices section).

There is also **no Quote → Invoice conversion** (the schema-only `sourceQuoteId` was removed in v2.9.58), so today the only buyer-facing document in the app is an invoice at Pro-forma status, with every unit price typed by hand.

## 2. How the pricing maths constrains the document (must be understood before design)

`cQteLine()` (`index.html:14930`) builds **landed = cost + freight + insurance + duty (+ DG surcharge)** per line, and the per-line selling price is **landed × (1 + markup%)** (`qteSellTotals()` `14971`); the three overhead components (origin, destination, FPM admin) are added **at cost, never marked up**. So:

- A per-line selling price **already contains** the international freight, insurance and destination duty for that line. A customer document that *also* listed "freight" as a separate line would double-charge unless the lines were re-split — which changes the engine's presentation and **exposes the markup on freight** (forwarder rates are discoverable, so a buyer can compare the freight line to a market quote). That is the main way a "separate freight line" leaks margin.
- The **only** charges that are genuinely separate in the maths today are the three overhead components. "Local freight and taxes are known" maps onto the existing **Destination Charges** (and, if used, Origin Charges / Admin) per-quote overrides (`REQ/SPEC-QTE-002`).

## 3. Decision on charge lines (operator confirmed: enough as is)

The customer document prints, after the product lines, **only the overhead components that are non-zero**, each as its own line with a buyer-facing label: *Origin charges*, *Destination charges (local freight & taxes)*, *Handling & admin*. International freight, insurance and duty stay **inside the per-line selling prices** and are never itemised. Free-form extra charge lines (any description, any amount) are Phase 2 because they need a new stored field.

## 4. Requirements (Phase 1)

**REQ-QTE5-001 — Separate entry points, honest names.** There are **two** places that open the internal sheet and both are changed: the Quote modal's Preview button (`#qt-prev-btn`, `index.html:3037`, shown only for saved quotes, hidden for a new one `15052`/`15083`) and the Quote **list row** icon button (`index.html:15016`, calls `prevQte()`). The modal button is relabelled **"Internal costing sheet"**; the list-row icon gets the tooltip "Internal costing sheet (not for the client)" and a second icon/label **"Customer quote"** is added beside it. The modal gains a **"Customer quote"** button with **the same visibility rule as Preview** (saved quotes only). The internal sheet's `<title>` and a print-visible banner read **"INTERNAL — contains supplier and cost information. Do not send to the client."**

**REQ-QTE5-002 — Source of the document: the SAVED record, never the live form.** `prevQteId()` renders the saved `DB.qt` record (`index.html:16147`), and so does the customer document. If the Quote modal holds **unsaved edits** (the form state differs from the state captured when the modal opened — fields, status, and every line), clicking *Customer quote* first shows: "You have unsaved changes. The customer quote shows the saved version — Save first?" with **Save and continue** / **Continue with saved version** / **Cancel**. This prevents a buyer receiving a price different from the one on screen.

**REQ-QTE5-003 — Customer document contents.** Header from `buildPdfHeader()` (`11003`) — logo/name, address, branding email/phone, reg/VAT — plus the company email from `AS.email` when branding has no email (the old sheet printed it); title **QUOTATION**; quote number, date, **Valid until** (only when set), client, and `Shipping: <freight mode>` (LCL / FCL 20GP / FCL 40HQ) under the client line (open §8 b); a product table with exactly: **Description, Qty, UOM, Unit price, Line total**; the non-zero overhead lines (§3); a single **Total**; **Price basis** (REQ-QTE5-010); **Terms & conditions** from the new `AS.quoteTerms` setting (REQ-QTE5-010 — **not** `AS.terms`, which holds invoice payment wording such as "Payment due within 30 days of invoice date…" that does not belong on a quotation, `index.html:3282`/`13646`); bank details (`fmtBankHtml(AS.bank)`, `11038`) when configured, otherwise nothing; the company footer (`buildPdfFooter()` `11020`); a page footer with the quote number and "Page x of y" (invoice print-CSS technique).

**REQ-QTE5-004 — Prices the buyer can reconcile.** Per line: **selling price** S = `landed × (1 + effective markup%)` from the **live recomputation `cQte(q)`** (`index.html:14981`), not the stored `calc_*` fields (which are fixed at save time, `15617`). **Unit price** U = S ÷ qty (qty ≤ 0 treated as 1; fractional qty allowed), displayed to **4 decimal places with trailing zeros trimmed to a minimum of 2** (so 12.7 prints `12.70`, 12.7143 prints `12.7143`); **Line total** = `round2(displayed U × Qty)` — i.e. the line total is derived **from the printed unit price**, so a buyer multiplying the two columns always gets the printed line total (±0.005). Overhead lines and the **Total** are `round2` values and **Total = the sum of the printed line totals and charge lines**. Consequence, stated and bounded: the printed Total can differ from the engine's unrounded sell total by at most `Σ(0.00005 × qty) + 0.005 × (number of lines)` (about 25 cents on a 5,000-unit line); the difference is neither printed nor written back to the record.

**REQ-QTE5-005 — Currency: USD (operator to re-confirm).** The document is issued in USD, every amount carries a `USD` label, and no GBP total is printed. **The operator's answer was cut off after "use"** — read as USD; it must be confirmed before the SPEC starts (Barbados trade makes BBD or GBP plausible, and neither is supported). **The quote Currency field is not reliable** (`QTE-GAP-004`, `docs/known-gaps.md`): an Order-Request-converted quote fills line costs in the quote's own currency (`ordConvertToQuote()`, `index.html:3703-3712`) while the engine treats the Cost column as USD, so a non-USD-labelled quote may hold non-USD numbers. Therefore: **if the quote Currency is not USD the pre-send dialog (REQ-QTE5-006) shows a blocking warning** "This quote's currency is X. The engine prices in USD, so these amounts may not be USD — check the line costs before sending." with **Generate anyway** / **Cancel**; nothing is silently relabelled.

**REQ-QTE5-006 — One pre-send dialog, not a chain of confirms.** Clicking *Customer quote* (after REQ-QTE5-002) opens an in-app modal (`#ov-qdoc`, placed after `#ov-qt` — all `.ov` share one z-index) listing, for the saved quote: **(1) the price-basis text** (pre-filled from `AS.priceBasis`, editable for this document only — REQ-QTE5-010); **(2) an "Include quote Notes" checkbox**, shown only when the quote has Notes, **unchecked by default** (Notes have always been internal-by-habit); **(3) a warnings list**, each item informational with the offending line named: a line description mentioning a Supplier's name (case-insensitive, `DB.sup` names of ≥ 3 characters) or the SKU of its linked catalogue item; no validity date ("open-ended"); a line with no cost basis (cost 0); a line with a selling price of 0; the currency warning (REQ-QTE5-005); quote status Declined or Expired; a live total that differs from the saved `calc_sellUSD` by more than 0.01 (rates or catalogue changed since saving — shows both figures). A quote whose **Total is 0 cannot be generated** (blocking message). Buttons: **Generate** / **Cancel**. Because the document is opened from the Generate click itself, the browser's pop-up rule is satisfied. **If `window.open` returns null** the operator sees "Pop-ups are blocked — allow pop-ups for this page, then click Generate again" and the dialog stays open. The leak check is a **backstop, not a guarantee** (it does not catch abbreviations or brand names); the REQ states this in the user guide.

**REQ-QTE5-007 — No internal content can appear.** The customer document contains **no** supplier name, SKU/catalogue reference, cost, freight, insurance, duty, landed value, markup/margin percentage, DG surcharge amount, per-line version note, line `note`, or price-history data. The only line text printed is the line **Description**. Tested with a sentinel quote: every internal field holds a unique sentinel string, the description holds a distinct *printable* sentinel which **must** appear, and none of the others may.

**REQ-QTE5-008 — No status or data side effect; escaping.** Generating the document changes no record (status, notes, anything) — the operator sets status to Sent themselves; a one-line hint after generation reminds them. Every free-text value is `san()`-escaped; the page title is `<quote number> — Quotation`; the document is an in-memory blob like the other previews.

**REQ-QTE5-009 — Docs and assistant.** `AI_SYSTEM_PROMPT` and `docs/user-guide.md` explain the two documents, the internal-only warning, the charge lines, the dialog and its warnings, that international freight, insurance and import duty are **inside** the line prices, the rounding rule, and that a reprint reflects today's Settings, rates and catalogue until Phase 2 stores issued copies; changelog, three version strings, tracker rows.

**REQ-QTE5-010 — Two new local Settings fields, wired end to end.** `AS.priceBasis` ("Price basis") and `AS.quoteTerms` ("Quote terms & conditions") are new local settings values (stored in `AS`, key `K.as`; never on the quote; no quote schema change). The existing settings save is **not** automatic: `saveCfg()` (`index.html:13747`) rebuilds `AS` from a fixed whitelist of eight fields and **drops any other key**, `rCfg()` (`13641`) loads only those, and restoring a backup replaces `AS` wholesale (`data.company`, `~14202`). Therefore the REQ requires: two inputs in the Settings form, `rCfg()` populating them, `saveCfg()` carrying them, backup/restore carrying them (and an old backup lacking them leaving them blank, never `undefined` text on the document), and tests for each. Blank `AS.priceBasis` omits the Price-basis line; blank `AS.quoteTerms` omits the Terms section. The placeholder shown in Settings for Price basis describes what the engine actually prices: "Line prices include international freight, insurance and import duty. Origin, destination and handling charges are itemised separately." — **no Incoterm** (the maths are not CIF: insurance is a % of cost+freight, and destination duty is included). The per-document edit in the dialog is not remembered anywhere (no session carry-over) and the Settings value is the only default. GDPR: both fields are business text in local storage and the existing backup JSON; no personal data.

## 5. Out of scope for Phase 1 (disclosed, not forgotten)
- **A stored copy of what was sent** (Phase 2, §7). Until then a reprint reflects *today's* Settings, rates and catalogue — say so in the user guide.
- Free-form extra charge lines; per-line "hide this line's price"; Incoterm field; discounts; multiple options/alternatives per quote; e-mailing the quote from the app; a Quote → Invoice conversion.
- Changing any pricing maths. **Existing `prevQteDoc` tests** (`tests/run.js:1324`, `2676`, `14720`, `14731`) assert the internal sheet's content: they stay valid except where they assert the exact `<title>`/header, which the build adjusts and lists.

## 7. Phase 2 (separate REQ/SPEC): stored issued copies
`quotes.issued_docs` (nullable `jsonb`, additive migration — all four Cloud Data touchpoints: insert, update, refresh mapping with the bare `!= null` guard, migration function — plus `schema-migration-reviewer`): an **Issue quote** action stores an immutable snapshot (`{n, issuedAt, quoteNum, clientName, lines[{desc, qty, uom, unit, total}], charges[], total, currency, termsText, bankText, headerText, validUntil}`) so a reprint is byte-for-byte what the buyer received regardless of later Settings/catalogue/rate changes; re-issuing increments `n`; the quote status moves to Sent on issue (with confirm); a read-only "Issued copies" list on the quote. GDPR: the snapshot holds the buyer name already on the quote and company/bank text already in Settings; no new external transmission. Further Phase 2 points the gate raised: `saveQte()` rebuilds the quote from a literal (`index.html:~15600`), so `issuedDocs` must be carried forward explicitly on every save; quotes are Sheets-synced (`'qt'` in the sync entity list, `FIELD_MAPS.qt`) — `issued_docs` is **not** mapped and survives a pull via `mergePulledWithLocal()`'s `Object.assign({}, local, pulled)` shape, which the SPEC must cite; backup/restore carries `DB.qt` as-is. **GDPR correction:** for a Cloud-Data-migrated quote the snapshot **is** transmitted to Supabase (buyer name and commercially sensitive pricing) — the same exposure the quote's `lines` already have; an immutable snapshot also cannot be erased through the app (SEC-GAP-022 — no GDPR erasure path on Cloud Data exists today), to be disclosed. Open: size limit per quote; whether to also store the rendered HTML.

## 9. Risks (stated plainly)
- **Per-line selling prices still reveal structure.** A buyer comparing lines can spot inconsistent markups, benchmark against known market prices (these are commodity products), or compare unit prices across quote versions with different quantities (freight is allocated per CBM, so bulky lines carry more freight). Showing fewer, rounder prices or a single total hides more; per-line is the operator's decision.
- A separate freight line (if later added) exposes freight markup — hence §3.
- Reprints are not reproducible until Phase 2.
