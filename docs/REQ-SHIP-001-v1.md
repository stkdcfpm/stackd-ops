# REQ-SHIP-001 — Auto-created Shipment record with a progressive trade-document checklist, triggered on Invoice → Paid

**Status:** v1 — drafted, self-reviewed (§8), all three originally-open questions resolved (§5). Independent review round 1: **FAIL**, 3 blocking + 4 advisory findings, fixed (§9). Round 2 (confirmatory): **CONDITIONAL PASS**, 2 new findings introduced by round 1's own fixes, fixed (§9b). Round 3 (confirmatory): **CONDITIONAL PASS**, no correctness defects — 1 documentation-consistency fix + 2 explicit disclosures (§9c). **Requirements-gate complete — ready for spec-gate.**
**Type:** New entity-lifecycle feature. Extends the existing Shipment entity (`DB.sh`) with a new structured field and a new auto-creation trigger sourced from Invoice. Touches `index.html` and `supabase/migrations/` (Shipment's Cloud Data table needs one new column). No new external dependency.

---

## 0. How this REQ came to be scoped this way

Operator's original ask, verbatim intent: *"for a paid invoice, I want to trigger the creation of a shipment record that has a lifecycle like the invoice's draft to complete, that will allow me to add the details of the shipment as I get them — like the Purchase Invoice, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, customs docs, certificates and Dangerous Goods docs — as an operator captures them over time."* Also asked that this be evaluated against real shipping/trade-compliance expertise to be "complete and robust" and to close "gaps in the market," not just satisfy the literal words of the ask.

This session has no dedicated shipping/trade-compliance reviewer agent — only software-correctness gates (`requirements-gate`, `spec-gate`, `build-gate`, `security-gate`). The honest way to ground this REQ in real domain practice, short of hiring a customs broker, is: (a) cite authoritative sources for the standard document set rather than working from training-data recall, and (b) have the operator — the actual domain expert running a real trade-intermediary business — confirm the resulting checklist before it ships, which is what §8's open questions ask for.

### 0.1 What was actually researched (with sources)

Standard ocean-freight import document set, confirmed across multiple carrier/logistics sources: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading (or sea waybill), Certificate of Origin are the near-universal core; a Verified Gross Mass (VGM) declaration is also required for essentially all containerized cargo. ([iContainers — Shipping Document Checklist Generator](https://www.icontainers.com/help/shipping-document-checklist/), [Maersk — Important Shipping Documents](https://www.maersk.com/logistics-explained/shipping-documentation/2023/08/27/important-shipping-documents), [FreightAmigo — 2026 Guide: Essential Sea Freight Documents](https://www.freightamigo.com/en/blog/logistics/key-freight-documents-for-sea-freight-and-freight-forwarding-in-2026/))

**The VGM point is worth calling out specifically as a "gap in the market" catch, not a generic addition:** Verified Gross Mass declarations have been a mandatory SOLAS requirement for every containerized ocean shipment worldwide since 2016 — a genuinely universal, non-optional document that small-operator shipment-tracking tools frequently omit from their checklists because it isn't as well-known as a Bill of Lading or Commercial Invoice. Including it by default, always, regardless of destination, is a real, low-effort differentiator.

**FPM's actual trade lane matters here, and it's not what the test fixtures suggest.** `STACKD_CONTEXT.md` states FPM International sources from Chinese manufacturers and supplies Barbados today, expanding to UK-Nigeria/UK-Ghana/UK-India corridors — general goods (SIC 46190, non-specialised wholesale), not food/seafood (the "frozen tilapia" strings in this codebase's own test fixtures are arbitrary placeholder data, not a real commodity signal). Barbados-specific research surfaced two customs forms not on the operator's own list — a C-60 (customs value declaration) and a C-63 (item/weight/shipper/tariff-code declaration), alongside a CARICOM-format invoice requirement. ([trade.gov — Barbados Country Commercial Guide](https://trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/barbados-import-requirements-and-documentation), [Blue Horizon Shipping — Barbados Customs Regulations PDF](https://bluehorizonshipping.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/BARBADOS-CUSTOMS-REGULATIONS.pdf))

**This is exactly why the default checklist must not hardcode Barbados-specific forms** (§1.2 Decision 2 below) — the stated expansion to Nigeria/Ghana/India means a checklist hardcoded to today's one destination's customs paperwork becomes actively wrong, not just incomplete, the moment a second corridor opens. A competitor tool that bakes in one country's form set and silently misleads operators shipping elsewhere is itself a "gap in the market" this REQ should avoid recreating.

Dangerous Goods documentation (IMDG-compliant Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods) is its own, separate, conditional requirement — only relevant when a shipment actually carries DG cargo, which this codebase already tracks via the existing `dg` boolean field on Shipment.

## 1. Business context

### 1.1 What exists today (check-first)

- `DB.sh` (Shipment) already exists as a top-level entity, Cloud-Data-migrated (`REQ-CLOUD-007`, v2.9.80). Fields relevant here: `status` (physical transit status: Booked/Confirmed/In Transit/.../Delivered — `index.html:4495-4497`), `docsStatus` (a single free-text/dropdown paperwork-completeness field: Pending/In Progress/Complete, rendered via a fixed lookup at `index.html:12871`), `dg` (boolean, already tracks Dangerous Goods presence), `linkedInvs` (array of Invoice refs — the existing, already-bidirectional-in-spirit join between Shipment and Invoice).
- `DB.inv` (Invoice) has a `status` field driven by a manual operator dropdown (`inv-sm`: Draft/Pro-forma/Sent/Partially Paid/Paid/Cancelled — `index.html:1133`, `STATUS_ORDER` at `index.html:2960`). `saveInv()` (`index.html:8203`) already captures the pre-save status (`_invOldStatus`) and, after the save completes, logs a `status_changed` event (`index.html:8444`) whenever it actually changed — this is the existing, already-correct hook point for detecting a transition *into* Paid specifically (`_invOldStatus !== 'Paid' && inv.status === 'Paid'`), not just "is the invoice Paid" (which would also match on every subsequent unrelated save of an already-Paid invoice).
- `autoPos()` (`index.html:8572`) is the direct precedent for "one Invoice save event triggers creation of a different entity's record(s)," fired today only from `saveInv()`'s interactive path (`if(!EI.i) autoPos(inv);`, `index.html:8448`) — **never** from CSV import or Google Sheets pull, which write `DB.inv`/`DB.po` directly and bypass `saveInv()`/`autoPos()` entirely. This REQ's new trigger follows the identical scoping: **fires only from `saveInv()`'s interactive save path, never from bulk import or sync paths.** A CSV backfill of historical already-Paid invoices, or a Sheets pull that happens to change an invoice's status field, must not spawn Shipment records — the operator is not present to review what gets created, and `autoPos()` already established this exact boundary for a materially similar risk (see `PO-GAP-003`/`SH-GAP-003`'s own history of unbounded-record-creation bugs from exactly this class of oversight).
- `saveShp()` (`index.html:12915`) shows that `ref` is a manually-entered, non-blank (≥2 chars), globally-unique field (`vShp()`, `index.html:10352`) — there is no auto-numbering scheme for Shipment today (unlike `SUP-####`/`LI-####`/etc.). An auto-created Shipment must synthesize its own collision-free `ref` since no operator is present at creation time to type one in, mirroring `autoPos()`'s own `'PO-'+(inv.num||Date.now().toString(36))+'-'+(idx+1)` fallback-numbering pattern for exactly the same reason.
- `linkedInvs` is the only existing join between Shipment and Invoice, and it is **not required to be 1:1** — a real Shipment can (and in practice, per this entity's own design, does) consolidate multiple Invoices into one container/BL. This matters directly for idempotency (§1.2 Decision 3).

### 1.2 Design decisions

**Decision 1 — New structured field, `tradeDocs[]`, added to the Shipment schema; `docsStatus` becomes a computed rollup for any Shipment that has one, and stays manually-set (unchanged behavior) for every Shipment that predates this feature.**

```js
tradeDocs: [
  { id: uid(), type: 'Commercial Invoice', status: 'Pending', refNum: '', fileLocation: '', receivedDate: '', notes: '' },
  ...
]
```
- `status` per document: `'Pending' | 'Received' | 'N/A'`.
- `fileLocation`: free text — a link or path to where the actual file lives (a Google Drive URL, a network share path, an email reference), **not a file upload**. Per operator's own explicit scoping: no file storage anywhere in this codebase today (only Postgres tables via `supabase-js` — no Supabase Storage bucket use exists), and standing one up is a separate infrastructure decision this REQ does not make. This field is rendered through `san()` like every other free-text field in the app; it is not validated as a well-formed URL (an operator might reasonably record a shelf/folder location for a physical paper archive, not just a link), only sanitized for safe display.
- `docsStatus` computed rule, recalculated on every `tradeDocs` mutation, for any Shipment that has a `tradeDocs` array at all: every entry `Received` or `N/A` → `'Complete'`; at least one `Received` but not all → `'In Progress'`; none `Received` → `'Pending'`. A Shipment with no `tradeDocs` array (every record created before this feature ships) keeps today's exact behavior — `docsStatus` stays a plain manually-set field, no forced backfill, no migration run against existing records. This preserves `shpStatusClass()`'s existing render logic and `FIELD_MAPS.sh`'s existing Sheets-sync mapping for `docsStatus` completely unchanged; `tradeDocs` is additive.

**Decision 2 — Default seed list is destination-agnostic and universal; nothing destination-specific is hardcoded.**

Per §0.1's research: FPM's stated ICP is multi-corridor (Barbados today, UK-Nigeria/UK-Ghana/UK-India next), and Barbados-specific customs forms (C-60/C-63/CARICOM invoice) would be actively wrong defaults the moment a second corridor ships. The seeded default list, applied to every auto-created Shipment regardless of destination:

1. Purchase Invoice — **resolved with the operator as a genuinely separate document from Commercial Invoice below**: this is the supplier's own bill to FPM for the goods (received from the supplier, distinct from both `autoPos()`'s auto-generated Purchase Order — FPM's order *to* the supplier — and the Commercial Invoice below). Seeded as a plain checklist line like every other entry, `status: 'Pending'`, **no auto-population from the linked PO or Invoice record** — those are different documents and conflating them would misrepresent what's actually been received.
2. Commercial Invoice
3. Packing List
4. Bill of Lading
5. Certificate of Origin
6. Verified Gross Mass (VGM) Declaration
7. Insurance Certificate
8. Dangerous Goods Declaration — **seeded only when the Shipment's own `dg` field is `true`** (reusing the existing boolean, not inventing a parallel flag; if `dg` is toggled on manually after creation, this entry should be added at that point too — see REQ-SHIP-001e). This seeded entry carries `autoManaged: true` (a new boolean on the `tradeDocs` entry shape, absent/falsy on every operator-added custom line) so REQ-SHIP-001e's add/remove-on-toggle logic can find *the* auto-managed DG line unambiguously — matching purely by `type === 'Dangerous Goods Declaration'` would otherwise be ambiguous the moment an operator adds their own custom line with that same free-text label (independent review advisory A3).

The operator can freely add a custom document line (any free-text `type`) or remove/mark N/A any seeded line, per shipment — this is how destination-specific paperwork (Barbados' C-60/C-63, an ISPM-15 fumigation certificate for wood packaging, a phytosanitary certificate, an import permit) gets tracked without this codebase hardcoding a specific country's rules anywhere. A "save this shipment's current document set as a reusable preset for this lane" convenience is a plausible future enhancement but is explicitly **out of scope for v1** — noted here so it isn't silently forgotten, not built now.

**Decision 3 (revised after independent review round 1 — see §9) — Trigger on `inv.status === 'Paid'` unconditionally, exactly like the existing FPM-funded-deposit-recovery block; idempotency is carried entirely by the `linkedInvs` scan, not by a status-transition guard.**

The original v1 draft gated this on detecting a Draft→Paid *transition* via `_invOldStatus`. Independent review (finding B3) correctly identified that this actively excludes a brand-new Invoice saved directly with `status: 'Paid'` (a plausible real workflow — recording a historical or COD transaction) — `_invOldStatus` is unconditionally `null` on `saveInv()`'s create path (`index.html:8238`), and the original guard required it to be non-null.

The fix is not a special-case carve-out for the create path — it's adopting a better pattern that already exists two lines away in the same function. `saveInv()` already has a block, "Auto-recover FPM-funded deposits when invoice is marked Paid" (`index.html:8449-8472`), solving the *identical* problem this REQ has: "do something whenever an invoice's current status is Paid, without redoing it on every subsequent save of an already-Paid invoice." It solves it by checking the *target* record for existing completion (`po.fpmFunded > 0 && !po.fpmRecovered`), not by tracking the invoice's own status history. This REQ now does the same: `autoCreateShipmentFromInvoice(inv)` fires whenever `inv.status === 'Paid'`, full stop — no `_invOldStatus` check, no `EI.i` check.

**Idempotency key, revised after round 2 review (finding C2) — an immutable `inv.id`-based tracking field, not the operator-facing `linkedInvs` (which is keyed by the mutable `inv.num`).** The deposit-recovery precedent's own idempotency flag (`po.fpmRecovered`) is stored on the target record and never depends on any *other* record's mutable field. The original revision of this Decision broke that property: it matched against `linkedInvs`, which (per Decision 3's own earlier verification) holds Invoice `num` — and `num` is an ordinary, renamable form field (`vInv()`, `index.html:10281-10296`, checks only non-blank and uniqueness, never immutability once Paid). Under the unconditional-on-`'Paid'` redesign, an operator renaming an already-shipped, already-Paid invoice's `num` for any unrelated reason (a typo fix) and saving again would cause the idempotency scan to look for the *new* `num` in `linkedInvs`, fail to find it (the existing Shipment still holds the *old* `num`), and spawn a duplicate Shipment — a new failure mode this redesign introduced, in the exact bug class (`SH-GAP-003`-style unbounded duplicate creation) this Decision's own text claims to guard against.

**Fix:** a new, dedicated, non-operator-facing field, `autoCreatedFromInvIds: []`, added to the Shipment schema (REQ-SHIP-001a) — an array of Invoice `id`s (never `num`s, never edited through any UI, never shown to the operator) that `autoCreateShipmentFromInvoice()` writes to and checks against. The idempotency check becomes: does any `DB.sh` record's `autoCreatedFromInvIds` already contain `inv.id`? If so, no-op — this half is immune to `num` renames entirely, since `id` never changes. The pre-existing `linkedInvs`-by-`num` check is kept as a **second, independent OR-condition** — not replaced — specifically to still catch the case Decision 3 originally named: a human manually pre-creating a Shipment and linking it via the ordinary `linkedInvs` free-text field *before* the trigger ever fires (that scenario has no `autoCreatedFromInvIds` entry to match against, since it was never auto-created). The new record's own `linkedInvs` is still seeded as `[inv.num]` (REQ-SHIP-001c, unchanged, operator-facing/display purpose only) — `autoCreatedFromInvIds` exists purely as the machine-reliable half of the check.

This also guards against the exact class of bug this codebase has been bitten by twice this session already (duplicate payment ledger rows, `SH-GAP-003`'s unbounded phantom Shipments from an unmatchable-blank-`ref` join): a human having already manually created the Shipment ahead of the trigger firing, and a Cloud Data refresh re-delivering a stale local copy of an already-Paid invoice.

This explicitly does **not** attempt to detect "this invoice's line items obviously belong to a Shipment that already exists for a *different*, related invoice" — that would require inferring shipment consolidation logic (which invoices ship together) this REQ has no reliable signal for. One Paid invoice with no already-linked Shipment always creates exactly one new Shipment; consolidating multiple invoices onto one Shipment afterward remains a manual operator action (editing `linkedInvs` on the auto-created record, or on a pre-existing one), unchanged from today.

**Verified by direct code read (not left as an open question): `linkedInvs` is keyed by Invoice `num`, not `id`.** `saveShp()` (`index.html:12919`) populates it from a free-text comma-separated operator input (`shf-invs`); the demo fixture holds `['DINV-0001']` (a display reference, not a `uid()`); and the code comment at `index.html:7324` states explicitly that `linkedInvs` "carries no FK field of any kind." The idempotency check (this Decision) and the new record's own `linkedInvs` value (REQ-SHIP-001c) both match against/write `inv.num`, matching this existing, already-live convention exactly.

**Decision 5 — A Settings toggle exists, defaulting on, and its off-state is surfaced persistently, not just at the point someone happens to open Settings.**

Resolved with the operator: a checkbox toggle (`SS.autoCreateShipmentOnPaid`, default `true`, stored alongside the existing `SS.fwdWebhook`-style settings) turns the whole trigger off. Given `CON-GAP-001`-class precedent for "a setting silently left off and forgotten," a bare Settings checkbox with no other signal is not enough — a `dev`/`ops` person who flips it off during testing, or an operator who disables it once for a one-off reason, can otherwise forget it's off indefinitely with zero visible consequence until they notice a Paid invoice quietly produced no Shipment. **Mitigation:** when the toggle is off, a persistent (not one-time-dismissible, not a toast) banner is shown on both the Invoices tab and the Shipments tab headers — `'⚠ Shipment auto-creation is OFF — Paid invoices will not create a Shipment record automatically. Turn back on in Settings → Integrations.'` — reusing this codebase's existing banner/notice rendering pattern (e.g. the GDPR disclosure note already present in the Integrations card, `index.html:757`) rather than inventing a new one. The banner disappears immediately once the toggle is switched back on; no snooze/dismiss state is introduced; it is not itself gated by Cloud Data or any migration marker (it's pure local UI state, `SS`-driven, exactly like `fwdWebhook`).

**Decision 4 (completed after independent review round 1 — see §9) — Cloud Data.** Shipment is already Cloud-Data-eligible (`REQ-CLOUD-007`). The auto-creation function must branch exactly like `autoPos()`/`saveShp()` do, matching **both** steps those precedents perform, not just the insert call (independent review finding B2 — the original v1 draft named only the insert, omitting the two steps below):
1. If `_sb && localStorage.getItem('st_sh_cloud_migration_ts')`: insert via `_sb.from('shipments').insert(shRow).select().single()` (requiring a new `trade_docs jsonb` column on the `shipments` table — a small, additive Supabase migration, no backfill needed since it's a new nullable column), **then overwrite the new record's temporary `uid()` with the real Postgres id** (`newShp.id = result.data.id;`, exactly as `saveShp()` does at `index.html:12959` and `autoPos()` does at `index.html:8599` for its own inserts), **then `await refreshShFromSupabase();`** (matching `saveShp()`'s own call at `index.html:12960` and `autoPos()`'s at `index.html:8601`). Skipping either step leaves `DB.sh` — and this REQ's own idempotency scan on the very next invoice save — working off a stale local shape that doesn't match what Supabase actually holds.
2. Else: push directly to local `DB.sh`/`sv(K.sh, DB.sh)`.

Per `CLAUDE.md`'s standing Cloud Data conventions: gate on the entity's own migration marker, not bare `_sb` truthiness, and confirm `tradeDocs` round-trips through `refreshShFromSupabase()` without the `!= null ? val : undefined` key-presence trap already documented as a recurring bug class in this codebase.

**Decision 7 (added after independent review round 2 — see §9b, finding C1) — `refreshShFromSupabase()` and `migrateShToSupabase()` must both be extended to carry `tradeDocs` **and** `autoCreatedFromInvIds`; as originally drafted, Decision 4 alone would have the mandatory post-insert refresh immediately strip the data it just seeded.**

Round 2 independent review found that mandating `refreshShFromSupabase()` (in REQ-SHIP-001h's own fix for B2) creates a new problem: that function (`index.html:6192-6202`) rebuilds `DB.sh` entirely via `.map()` from a fixed field list that does not include `trade_docs`/`tradeDocs` (nor `auto_created_from_inv_ids`/`autoCreatedFromInvIds`, once that field existed after this Decision's own C2 fix) at all. Calling it immediately after every Cloud Data insert — exactly what this REQ requires — would strip both just-seeded fields before an operator ever sees them, for every Cloud-Data-migrated Shipment. Separately, `migrateShToSupabase()` (`index.html:7349-7357`, the one-time migration an operator runs to move existing local Shipments into Supabase) has the identical fixed-field insert payload with no `trade_docs`/`auto_created_from_inv_ids` keys — any operator who already has local auto-created Shipments with real `tradeDocs` progress *before* running that one-time migration would have it silently and permanently discarded.

**Fix, required in both functions, for both fields (round 3 review confirmed this narrative bullet list itself had drifted to name only `tradeDocs` after `autoCreatedFromInvIds` was introduced by this same Decision's C2 fix — corrected here; REQ-SHIP-001h's own formal requirement text was already correct):**
- `refreshShFromSupabase()`: add `if (row.trade_docs != null) s.tradeDocs = row.trade_docs;` **and** `if (row.auto_created_from_inv_ids != null) s.autoCreatedFromInvIds = row.auto_created_from_inv_ids;` to the per-row mapping — the `!= null` guard (not `!= null ? val : undefined`) follows this codebase's own documented convention for a field that's genuinely absent on legacy records, so a Shipment predating this feature (whose Supabase row has both columns `null`) does **not** get either assigned an empty array — the keys stay absent, matching REQ-SHIP-001a's "absent, not an empty array, for pre-existing records" rule and preserving AC-8's "no computed `docsStatus` override" guarantee for legacy records. Assigning `[]` instead of leaving the key absent would make Decision 1's rollup rule vacuously report `'Complete'` for a legacy record with zero tracked documents — a real, distinct bug this exact wording avoids.
- `migrateShToSupabase()`: add `trade_docs: Array.isArray(s.tradeDocs) ? s.tradeDocs : null` **and** `auto_created_from_inv_ids: Array.isArray(s.autoCreatedFromInvIds) ? s.autoCreatedFromInvIds : null` to the insert payload, mirroring the function's own existing `linked_invs` coercion pattern immediately above it in the same object literal.

**Confirmed compatible with Shipment's separate Google Sheets sync path (independent review advisory A4, traced and verified, not left assumed):** `FIELD_MAPS.sh` (`index.html:4804`) has no `tradeDocs` entry, so `unmapRec('sh', ...)` only ever returns the fields it knows about, and `mergePulledWithLocal()` (`index.html:4847-4854`) does `Object.assign({}, localMatch, pulledMapped)` — an unmapped local field like `tradeDocs` survives a Sheets pull untouched. No change needed to the sync path; stated here so it's a verified fact in the REQ rather than an unstated assumption.

**Decision 6 (added after independent review round 1 — see §9, finding B1, the most severe finding of this round) — `saveShp()` must be modified to explicitly preserve `tradeDocs` across every edit; without this fix, the feature silently destroys its own data on first use.**

Independent review found, by direct code read, that `saveShp()` (`index.html:12915-12969`) builds a brand-new flat object literal purely from the edit modal's own form fields (`index.html:12920-12940`) and then does a full replace on edit — `DB.sh[idx]=shp` (local branch) or an equivalent `.update()` (Cloud Data branch) — with **zero preservation** of any field the form doesn't know about. This is a different, more dangerous pattern than `saveInv()`'s own edit path, which explicitly carries forward `pos`/`buyerApprovedAt`/`linkedQuoteId`/etc. from the existing record (`index.html:8244-8250`) specifically because those fields aren't on the Invoice form either. Since no Shipment edit-modal form field for `tradeDocs` exists today (and per REQ-SHIP-001f, the exact UI for editing it is a spec-gate call, likely a separate control from the main edit modal, not a hidden form field on it), **the very first time an operator edits any field on an auto-created Shipment through the normal "Edit Shipment" modal — correcting an ETA, adding a vessel name, or exactly the DG-checkbox toggle this REQ's own REQ-SHIP-001e describes — the entire trade-document checklist would be silently wiped, with no warning, no error, no toast.** This would have defeated the feature's entire stated purpose ("track document collection over time") the moment it was used as intended. This is exactly the "direct-mutation-bypasses-preservation" bug class `CLAUDE.md` documents recurring across this codebase's history (Contact/Line Item Cloud Data mutations bypassing migration-marker checks, `saveQte()`'s Contact-conversion bypass, etc.) — the same root cause, a new case.

**Fix, mirroring `saveInv()`'s existing pattern exactly (round 3 review note: this bullet originally named only `tradeDocs`, predating Decision 3's later introduction of `autoCreatedFromInvIds` via finding C2 — corrected here for consistency with REQ-SHIP-001e2's already-correct formal text, which covers both fields):** `saveShp()`'s edit path (`EI.sh` truthy) must, immediately after locating the existing record and before building the replacement `shp`/`shRow` object, carry both fields forward: `shp.tradeDocs = existing.tradeDocs || [];` and `shp.autoCreatedFromInvIds = existing.autoCreatedFromInvIds || [];` — for both the local-write and Cloud Data branches (the Cloud Data `shRow` payload must include `trade_docs: shp.tradeDocs` and `auto_created_from_inv_ids: shp.autoCreatedFromInvIds` on an update, or the same wipe happens server-side instead). This preservation applies **unconditionally on every edit**, not just for auto-created Shipments — a manually-created Shipment that later gains `tradeDocs` entries via REQ-SHIP-001f's own CRUD functions must survive an unrelated field edit exactly the same way.

### 1.3 GDPR / security note

No new PII category is introduced — `tradeDocs[]` contains shipment/document metadata (document type, a reference number, a free-text location string, a date), not personal data beyond what Shipment already carries (forwarder name/email, already covered by existing gaps `SEC-GAP-002`/`CON-GAP-001`-class handling). `fileLocation` being a free-text field that *could* contain a sensitive internal path or a shared-drive link with its own access controls is a mild, low-severity consideration — no different in kind from `notes` fields already present on every entity in this app — not a new category of risk requiring new mitigation.

## 2. Requirements

### REQ-SHIP-001a — New Shipment schema fields: `tradeDocs[]` and `autoCreatedFromInvIds[]`
Both added to `DB.sh` records. Absent (`undefined`/not present) on any Shipment created before this feature ships — no forced migration, no backfill. `docsStatus` remains a plain field on such records, computed nowhere. `autoCreatedFromInvIds` (Decision 3's revised idempotency key, §9b) is never rendered or editable in any UI — a purely internal bookkeeping field, distinct from the pre-existing, operator-facing `linkedInvs`.

### REQ-SHIP-001b — New function: `autoCreateShipmentFromInvoice(inv)` (revised per Decision 3/§9, §9b)
Called from `saveInv()`, placed alongside the existing "Auto-recover FPM-funded deposits when invoice is marked Paid" block (`index.html:8449-8472`) — same trigger condition, same location in the function, immediately after that block or immediately before it (implementation's call; both fire under the identical `inv.status === 'Paid'` condition so the "Invoice saved" toast at `index.html:8446` always fires first, matching `autoPos()`'s own toast-ordering precedent). Fires whenever `inv.status === 'Paid'` — **no `_invOldStatus` check, no `EI.i` check**. Never called from `processImportRecords()`/`processImport('inv')`, `pullAll()`, or any Cloud Data refresh path — matching `autoPos()`'s own established scope boundary (§1.1); all three of these write `DB.inv` directly and never invoke `saveInv()` (verified by independent review, `index.html:10572-10616`, `10893+`, `4956-4998`). Bails immediately, before the idempotency check, if `SS.autoCreateShipmentOnPaid === false` (REQ-SHIP-001k) — no toast, no side effect of any kind. Otherwise performs the idempotency check (Decision 3, revised §9b — scan `DB.sh` for an existing record whose `autoCreatedFromInvIds` contains `inv.id` **OR** whose `linkedInvs` contains `inv.num`); if either matches, does nothing (no toast, no duplicate, no error — a silent, correct no-op, mirroring how `autoPos()` silently returns when `cnt` is 0, and how the FPM-deposit-recovery block silently no-ops when every candidate PO already has `fpmRecovered` set).

### REQ-SHIP-001c — Synthesized `ref` and default field values
The new Shipment record: `ref` synthesized as `'SHP-' + (inv.num || Date.now().toString(36))`, with a collision-avoidance suffix (`-2`, `-3`, ...) appended only if that exact `ref` already exists in `DB.sh` — mirroring `autoPos()`'s numbering-fallback pattern, satisfying `vShp()`'s uniqueness rule even though this path bypasses `vShp()` entirely (same relationship `autoPos()` has to `vPO()`). `status` (physical transit) defaults to `'Booked'` — the first value in `RD_SHP_STATUS` (`index.html:4494`, corrected from this REQ's original incorrect citation of a nonexistent `SH_STATUSES` — independent review advisory A1). `linkedInvs` set to `[inv.num]` (operator-facing, display purpose, unchanged from Decision 3's original verification) and `autoCreatedFromInvIds` set to `[inv.id]` (internal idempotency key, Decision 3 revised §9b). `etd`/`eta`/`vessel`/`carrier`/`blNum`/`containerNum`/`forwarder`/`forwarderEmail` all blank — populated by the operator as they become known, exactly as asked. `dg` defaults to `false` unless a reliable signal exists on the Invoice to infer otherwise (none does today — leave `false`, operator toggles it manually, which per Decision 2 also adds the DG Declaration checklist line at that point).

### REQ-SHIP-001d — `tradeDocs[]` seeded per Decision 2
The universal seven-item default list (Purchase Invoice, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, VGM Declaration, Insurance Certificate), plus an eighth (Dangerous Goods Declaration) only when `dg` is `true` at creation time.

### REQ-SHIP-001e — Toggling `dg` after creation adds/removes the DG checklist line
If an operator edits an existing auto-created (or any) Shipment and changes `dg` from `false` to `true`, and no `tradeDocs` entry with `autoManaged: true` and `type === 'Dangerous Goods Declaration'` already exists, one is added (status `'Pending'`, `autoManaged: true`). Toggling `dg` back to `false` does **not** delete an existing DG checklist entry if it has already progressed (a `status` other than `'Pending'`, or has any `refNum`/`fileLocation`/`notes` filled in) — only a still-untouched, still-`'Pending'`, no-data, `autoManaged: true` DG line may be silently removed on `dg` becoming `false`, to avoid destroying an operator's already-recorded work. Matching by `autoManaged` rather than by `type` string alone means an operator's own custom-added line also happening to be labeled "Dangerous Goods Declaration" is never touched by this logic (independent review advisory A3).

### REQ-SHIP-001e2 — `saveShp()` must preserve `tradeDocs` AND `autoCreatedFromInvIds` across every edit (Decision 6, independent review finding B1 — the most severe finding of round 1)
`saveShp()`'s edit path (`EI.sh` truthy) must set `shp.tradeDocs = existing.tradeDocs || [];` and `shp.autoCreatedFromInvIds = existing.autoCreatedFromInvIds || [];` immediately after locating the existing record, before building the replacement object, for both the local-write and Cloud Data branches (the Cloud Data `shRow` update payload must include both `trade_docs: shp.tradeDocs` and `auto_created_from_inv_ids: shp.autoCreatedFromInvIds` — see REQ-SHIP-001h, which needs a Supabase column for each). Without this fix, any edit to any field on a Shipment carrying `tradeDocs` — through the pre-existing "Edit Shipment" modal, which has no form field for either of these — silently discards the entire checklist (and, separately, the idempotency tracking that prevents a future duplicate). This is not optional or deferrable to implementation judgment; it is a hard correctness requirement, verified in AC-14/AC-15 below.

### REQ-SHIP-001f — Per-document CRUD on `tradeDocs[]`
New functions to add a custom document line, edit one (status/refNum/fileLocation/receivedDate/notes), remove one, mirroring this codebase's existing per-line-item edit patterns (e.g. Shipment's own `editShp()`/`saveShp()` shape, or Order Request line editing) rather than inventing a new interaction pattern. Exact UI placement/layout is a spec-gate/implementation call.

**Explicit carry-forward (round 3 independent review flagged this brevity deliberately, not as a silent pass):** whatever concrete function shape spec-gate settles on for this CRUD, it must be held to the exact same standard REQ-SHIP-001e2 imposes on `saveShp()` — full preservation of every other Shipment field (including the other half of `tradeDocs` itself when editing one entry, `autoCreatedFromInvIds`, and everything else on the record) on every mutation, never a full-object-replace built from a partial field set. B1 was this exact bug class; a spec that re-introduces it in this feature's own CRUD layer because "the UI details are TBD" would be repeating the mistake this REQ spent two review rounds fixing elsewhere. Spec-gate must include this preservation guarantee as an explicit, testable requirement for whatever concrete functions it specifies here, not treat it as inherited for free.

### REQ-SHIP-001g — `docsStatus` computed rollup
Recomputed per Decision 1's rule on every `tradeDocs` mutation (add/edit/remove/status-change), for any Shipment that has a `tradeDocs` array. Never recomputed for a Shipment with no `tradeDocs` array — that Shipment's `docsStatus` stays exactly as manually set today.

### REQ-SHIP-001h — Cloud Data (revised per Decision 4/§9 finding B2, and Decision 7/§9b finding C1)
New `trade_docs jsonb` **and** `auto_created_from_inv_ids jsonb` nullable columns on the Supabase `shipments` table (one migration file). `autoCreateShipmentFromInvoice()` and the new per-document CRUD functions (REQ-SHIP-001f) branch on `st_sh_cloud_migration_ts` exactly as every other Shipment mutation site already does. On the Cloud Data insert branch, after `_sb.from('shipments').insert(shRow).select().single()` succeeds: overwrite the record's temporary `uid()` with the real returned id, then `await refreshShFromSupabase();` — both steps, matching `autoPos()` (`index.html:8599`/`8601`) and `saveShp()` (`index.html:12959`/`12960`) exactly; neither step is optional.

**`refreshShFromSupabase()` and `migrateShToSupabase()` must both be extended (Decision 7) — required, not optional, since the first of these is called unconditionally immediately after every auto-creation insert per this same requirement:**
- `refreshShFromSupabase()` (`index.html:6192-6202`): add `if (row.trade_docs != null) s.tradeDocs = row.trade_docs;` and `if (row.auto_created_from_inv_ids != null) s.autoCreatedFromInvIds = row.auto_created_from_inv_ids;` to the per-row mapping — the bare `!= null` guard (never `!= null ? val : undefined`) so a legacy Shipment's `null` column value leaves the key **absent**, not set to `[]` (which would make Decision 1's `docsStatus` rollup rule vacuously report `'Complete'` for a record with zero tracked documents — a distinct bug this exact wording prevents, per AC-8's "no computed override" guarantee for pre-existing records).
- `migrateShToSupabase()` (`index.html:7349-7357`): add `trade_docs: Array.isArray(s.tradeDocs) ? s.tradeDocs : null` and `auto_created_from_inv_ids: Array.isArray(s.autoCreatedFromInvIds) ? s.autoCreatedFromInvIds : null` to the insert payload, mirroring the function's own existing `linked_invs` coercion pattern in the same object literal — without this, an operator's pre-existing local `tradeDocs` progress would be silently and permanently discarded by the one-time migration to Supabase.

### REQ-SHIP-001i — `AI_SYSTEM_PROMPT` update
Per the standing "mandatory on every version" rule — describe the new auto-creation trigger and the trade-document checklist so the chat assistant answers accurately if asked "does marking an invoice Paid do anything else" or "how do I track shipping documents."

### REQ-SHIP-001j — Toast / operator visibility on auto-creation
When `autoCreateShipmentFromInvoice()` actually creates a new record (not the idempotent no-op case, and not the toggle-off bail-out), the operator sees a toast (`'Shipment ' + ref + ' auto-created — add details as they become available'`) so the new record isn't silently invisible after an invoice save, matching this codebase's existing `autoPos()` precedent (`toast(cnt+' PO'+(cnt!==1?'s':'')+' auto-generated')`).

### REQ-SHIP-001k — Settings toggle + persistent off-state banner
New `SS.autoCreateShipmentOnPaid` boolean, default `true`, editable via a new checkbox in Settings → Integrations (alongside the existing Forwarder Webhook URL field), persisted via the existing `sv(K.ss, SS)` pattern. Per Decision 5: while `false`, a persistent (non-dismissible, non-toast) banner renders at the top of both the Invoices and Shipments tab views, and disappears the moment the toggle is switched back on. The banner is pure local UI state — not migrated, not synced, not gated on Cloud Data.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1:** Saving an Invoice with `status: 'Paid'` (whether transitioning from a different status, or set directly on a brand-new invoice — both cases, per Decision 3's revised design), with no existing Shipment linking it, creates exactly one new Shipment record with the seeded default `tradeDocs[]` (7 entries; 8 if `dg` is true), a synthesized unique `ref`, and `linkedInvs` containing this invoice's `num`.
- **AC-1b:** Creating a brand-new Invoice directly with `status: 'Paid'` (never having been Draft/Sent/etc. first) also creates a Shipment — this is the case the original v1 draft's `_invOldStatus` guard would have missed (independent review finding B3), now covered by Decision 3's unconditional-on-status-value redesign.
- **AC-2:** Saving an already-Paid Invoice again (already has a linked Shipment) creates no second Shipment.
- **AC-3:** Saving an Invoice whose status is `'Paid'`, when a Shipment already exists with this invoice's `id` in its `autoCreatedFromInvIds` (regardless of whether this save represents a status change or not), creates no second Shipment (idempotency).
- **AC-3b (independent review round 2, finding C2 — a genuine new failure mode the round-1 fix introduced):** auto-create a Shipment for an Invoice, then rename that Invoice's `num` (a plain, unrestricted, editable field — `vInv()` never locks it post-Paid) and save again for an unrelated reason. No second Shipment is created — the idempotency check matches on the invoice's immutable `id` via `autoCreatedFromInvIds`, not on the renamed `num`. This is the specific scenario that would have produced a silent duplicate under the original round-1 fix (which matched only against `linkedInvs`-by-`num`).
- **AC-4:** CSV-importing an Invoice record with `status: 'Paid'` set directly (bypassing `saveInv()`) creates no Shipment.
- **AC-5:** A Google Sheets pull (`pullAll()`) that updates an Invoice's status to Paid creates no Shipment.
- **AC-6:** The synthesized `ref` never collides with an existing Shipment `ref` — verified with a test that pre-seeds a colliding `ref` and confirms the suffix logic produces a unique one.
- **AC-7:** `docsStatus` is `'Pending'` immediately after auto-creation (all seeded docs `'Pending'`), moves to `'In Progress'` once at least one is marked `'Received'` (with others still `'Pending'`), and to `'Complete'` once every entry is `'Received'` or `'N/A'`.
- **AC-8:** A Shipment created before this feature (no `tradeDocs` array) is unaffected — its `docsStatus` remains exactly as stored, no computed override, no crash on render.
- **AC-9:** Setting `dg: true` on a Shipment with no existing DG checklist line adds one; setting `dg: false` afterward removes it only if it is still untouched/`'Pending'` with no recorded data, and leaves it in place otherwise.
- **AC-10:** Cloud Data: with Shipment migrated, auto-creation inserts via Supabase including the new `trade_docs`/`auto_created_from_inv_ids` columns; without migration, it writes to local `DB.sh` only — both paths tested.
- **AC-11:** `fileLocation` is rendered through `san()` — a value containing `<script>`/`"` does not break out of its containing markup (mirrors this session's own `SEC-GAP-021` fix pattern; verified by test, not assumed).
- **AC-12:** With `SS.autoCreateShipmentOnPaid` set to `false`, an Invoice transitioning to Paid creates no Shipment and shows no toast; the Invoices and Shipments tab views both render the persistent off-state banner; switching the toggle back to `true` makes the banner disappear on next render with no page reload required.
- **AC-13:** With the toggle at its default (`true`, or unset — an existing operator's `SS` predates this field), auto-creation behaves exactly as AC-1 with no banner shown.
- **AC-14 (independent review finding B1 — the primary new coverage this round requires):** Auto-creating a Shipment via AC-1, then editing that Shipment through the normal `saveShp()` path to change an unrelated field (e.g. `vessel`), preserves `tradeDocs` unchanged — it is not wiped, not reset to `[]`, not `undefined`.
- **AC-15:** Same as AC-14 but specifically via the DG toggle path (REQ-SHIP-001e) — flipping `dg` on an auto-created Shipment through `saveShp()` both applies REQ-SHIP-001e's add/remove logic correctly AND preserves every other pre-existing `tradeDocs` entry untouched.
- **AC-16:** A manually-created Shipment (not auto-created) that later gains `tradeDocs` entries via REQ-SHIP-001f's CRUD functions also survives an unrelated `saveShp()` edit with `tradeDocs` intact — the preservation fix is unconditional, not auto-creation-specific.
- **AC-17 (independent review round 2, finding C1):** With Shipment Cloud-Data-migrated, auto-creating a Shipment and then immediately reading it back (as `autoCreateShipmentFromInvoice()`'s own mandatory `refreshShFromSupabase()` call does per REQ-SHIP-001h) preserves the seeded `tradeDocs` and `autoCreatedFromInvIds` — neither is stripped by the refresh. This is the specific regression the round-1 fix for B2 (mandating the refresh call) would have introduced without Decision 7's corresponding fix to `refreshShFromSupabase()` itself.
- **AC-18:** A legacy Shipment predating this feature (Supabase row has `trade_docs: null`) round-trips through `refreshShFromSupabase()` with `tradeDocs` left **absent** (not set to `[]`) — `docsStatus` for that record is not recomputed and shows no spurious `'Complete'` (AC-8's guarantee, now also verified across the Cloud Data read path specifically, not just the local one).
- **AC-19:** Running `migrateShToSupabase()` on a local Shipment that already has real `tradeDocs`/`autoCreatedFromInvIds` progress preserves both after migration — verified by reading the record back via `refreshShFromSupabase()` post-migration.
