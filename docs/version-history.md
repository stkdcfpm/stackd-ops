Acknowledged, not actively mitigated: consolidating 8-10 executions' worth of work into one narrows the margin against Apps Script's 6-minute execution-time quota — judged low-risk at this project's current data volumes, logged as a monitored risk with a concrete revisit trigger (REQ-SYNC-002f).
